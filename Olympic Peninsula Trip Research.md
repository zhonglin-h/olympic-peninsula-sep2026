## Road Check — Day 2 (verified Sep 9, 2026)

Sources: [NPS Olympic Alerts & Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/conditions.htm) (page last updated Aug 27, 2026) and [WSDOT](https://wsdot.wa.gov/about/news/2026/long-weekend-and-overnight-closures-horizon-us-101-hoh-river-bridge-south-forks) (published Sep 2, 2026).

### All stops are paved-highway access — no gravel roads

| Stop | Access | Surface |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Aberdeen → Bellevue | US-12 → I-5 | Highway |

> [!warning] Don't overshoot at Quinault
> **South Shore Rd is closed at mile 8** (road washout). The spruce at mile 3.3 is well inside the open section, but this breaks the Quinault loop drive — you cannot continue around the lake. Turn around and return to US-101 the way you came. Also avoid **North Fork Rd** and **Graves Creek Rd** (both narrow gravel) — neither is on the route.

### US-101 Hoh River Bridge — on the route, open but slow

The bridge sits on US-101 between Forks and Ruby Beach, so Day 2 crosses it. Status:

- **Open on Sept 9–10.** Closures don't begin until Sept 14.
- **One-way alternating traffic at 25 mph since Jul 14, 2026**, controlled by temporary signals on both ends. Budget extra time in the 11:05 AM Ruby Beach leg — the itinerary's 1h30m Sequim→Ruby Beach estimate does not account for a signal wait.
- **There is no detour across the Hoh River.** Going around the east side of the peninsula adds 4+ hours.
- Upcoming full closures, for reference if the trip slips: nightly 10:30 PM–5:30 AM Sept 14–22, then all-day Sept 24–29, Oct 1–6, and Oct 8–13.

### Other closures — not on the route

- **Mora Rd** closed to all traffic Jul 8–Oct 15 (Rialto Beach unreachable). NPS explicitly suggests Ruby Beach as the substitute, which is what you're already doing.
- **Elwha / Olympic Hot Springs Rd** closed at Madison Falls (washout).
- **Hoh Rain Forest** is open, but you're skipping it — the 2-hour entrance queue note still holds.

### Day 1 correction

NPS now lists **Obstruction Point Rd as open** (still gravel, still not for RVs/trailers). The note below says to avoid it — that remains a reasonable call for the car, but it isn't closed. More importantly: **there is no potable water at Hurricane Ridge** during the 2026 utility construction, and the parking lot work runs through August with possible rolling closures. Carry all your water up.

### What it means for the plan

- **Dry both days.** Precip chance tops out at 12% anywhere on the route, and Thursday is 0–2% across the whole Day 2 loop. No rain gear needed beyond a shell.
- **Hurricane Ridge runs ~11°F colder than Sequim on Day 1 and ~15°F colder on Day 2**, and it's mostly sunny both days — the hike window is close to ideal. Wind is calm (2–7 mph), so no wind chill on the exposed ridge.
- **Layers still matter up top.** 63°F in sun at 5,000 ft with Thursday's daytime high only 54°F and falling through the afternoon; Thursday night on the ridge drops to 38°F.
- **The coast is the cool, grey part of Day 2.** Ruby Beach and Kalaloch sit at 59°F, mostly cloudy, with NW wind 9–14 mph off the water. Bring a windbreaker for the beach stops specifically — it will feel much colder than Lake Quinault (66°F) an hour later.
- **Patchy fog forecast for Sequim on Thursday night** — irrelevant to this itinerary since you're back in Bellevue, but worth knowing if plans slip a day.
- **No smoke or haze in any forecast**, which is not a given for September on the peninsula.
