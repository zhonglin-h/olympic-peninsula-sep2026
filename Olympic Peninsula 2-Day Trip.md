> [!note] Two calls to make on the road
> **Forks, ~12:25 PM** — if the Hoh bridge wait has put you badly behind, eat there instead (Sully's Drive-In, on 101 at the north end). After Forks there is no fallback until Quinault.
> **Beach 4** — dropping it gets you home ~8:50 PM instead of 9:25. It's already conditional on the tide, so it's the clean thing to cut.

- **Walk-in by design:** the Aberdeen Supercharger, Breakwater Seafoods, Sully's in Forks — the fallbacks take no reservation, which is the point.
- **Still unbooked:** Day 1 dinner in Port Angeles.
- **Check in advance, not a booking:** the Kalaloch tide table against the 3:10 PM Beach 4 slot, and whether your route to Port Angeles uses the Edmonds–Kingston ferry — no reservations, but a missed sailing costs ~40 min off the front of Day 1.

## Essentials

- **Layers** for the ridge (~15°F colder than Sequim) and a **windbreaker** for the Day 2 beaches
- **No food service and no potable water at Hurricane Ridge** — carry everything up
- **Reserve both Day 2 meals before leaving Sequim** — signal is unreliable from Lake Crescent to Quinault
- **Day 2 gets home ~9:25 PM** — let anyone waiting know; the last I-5 leg is after dark
- Skipped by design: Storm King **Mountain** (steep, exposed summit trail — Marymere Falls shares the trailhead and *is* on Day 2), and the Hoh Rainforest (long entrance queue)
- Road/weather recording: **360-565-3131** · [nps.gov/olym alerts](https://www.nps.gov/olym)
